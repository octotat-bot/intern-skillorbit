<!-- chunk id=jd-ml-001 | title=AI/ML engineer: typical responsibilities | topic=job-description | roles=ai_ml_engineer | sections=experience,projects | source=self-authored -->
Junior ML engineers prepare and explore datasets, engineer features, train and evaluate models, compare them with baselines, and help deploy them behind APIs or batch jobs. Increasingly they also build applications on large language models, including retrieval-augmented generation and prompt evaluation, and monitor models in production.

<!-- chunk id=jd-ml-002 | title=AI/ML engineer: required skills | topic=job-description | roles=ai_ml_engineer | sections=skills | source=self-authored -->
Commonly required: Python, machine learning fundamentals (supervised and unsupervised learning, overfitting, regularization), deep learning, at least one framework (scikit-learn plus TensorFlow or PyTorch), NumPy and pandas, statistics and linear algebra, and model evaluation (train/test splits, cross-validation, precision/recall). Frequently preferred: NLP or computer vision, LLMs and Hugging Face, Docker, MLOps practices, SQL and a cloud platform.

<!-- chunk id=jd-ml-003 | title=AI/ML engineer: why evaluation and deployment matter | topic=skill-gap | roles=ai_ml_engineer | sections=skills,projects | source=self-authored -->
Employers screen for evaluation rigor because a model is only useful if its reported performance holds on unseen data: mention baselines, validation strategy and the metric that fits the problem. Deployment experience (a Flask or FastAPI endpoint, a Docker image, a hosted demo) separates engineers from notebook-only work and is a frequent differentiator for entry-level candidates.

<!-- chunk id=jd-ml-004 | title=AI/ML engineer: portfolio project ideas | topic=projects | roles=ai_ml_engineer | sections=projects | source=self-authored -->
Strong ML portfolio projects show the full loop: a classifier on a real dataset with a documented baseline, error analysis and a deployed API; a fine-tuned transformer for text classification with a confusion matrix; a computer-vision model served in a small web app; or a RAG chatbot over a document set with an evaluation of answer quality. Report metrics and dataset sizes.

<!-- chunk id=jd-ml-005 | title=AI/ML engineer: example strong bullets | topic=bullets | roles=ai_ml_engineer | sections=projects,experience | source=self-authored -->
Examples of ML bullets: "Trained a gradient boosting churn model on 50,000 customers, improving recall from 0.61 to 0.78 over a logistic regression baseline." "Fine-tuned a DistilBERT classifier reaching 91% F1 on 12,000 support tickets." "Deployed the model as a FastAPI service in Docker, serving predictions in under 100 ms."
